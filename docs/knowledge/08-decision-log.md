| 2026-10-07 | Map marker actions (add, move, rename, delete, reorder) are optimistic: the app chooses the marker UUID (sent as `id` and as the `Idempotency-Key`), Room shows the change at once, a per-trip queue in the application scope sends changes in order, retries a dropped connection after 1 s and 2 s, then undoes it and shows a banner with Retry; a refusal is undone and shows the server's message. `ID_CONFLICT` on add and `NOT_FOUND` on delete count as done.
